What's Included

The event operating system, in one package.

CirclePing connects attendee access, ticketing, cashless purchases, vendor operations, event staffing and reporting into a single event experience.

01

NFC Wristbands

Every attendee receives a wristband for event entry and tap-to-pay purchases across vendors.

02

M-Pesa Ticketing

Ticketing platform with M-Pesa STK checkout and multi-QR delivery for attendee ticket access.

03

Cashless Vending

Attendees can pay any vendor with a single wristband tap, reducing dependence on physical cash.

04

Vendor POS

POS terminals are configured per vendor, or CirclePing can be installed on the vendor's own Android device.

05

Dedicated Event Staff

On-ground personnel support attendee entry at the gate and operations inside the venue.

06

Organizer Dashboard

Live visibility into entries, sales and settlement gives organizers one operational view of the event.

Commercial Structure

Clear costs. No hidden platform fees.

Organizer and vendor costs are separated so each party can understand exactly what they are responsible for.

Organizer Costs

Billed to the organizer. Wristband cost can be folded into the ticket price.
ItemRateNotes
NFC wristband KSH 100 / attendee Recommended to be folded into ticket price. At 1,000–1,500 pax: KSH 100,000–150,000 carried by attendees.
Event staff KSH 1,000 / person 20 dedicated members: 10 at the gate and 10 inside. Total KSH 20,000 for the day/evening.
Ticketing & cashless platform Included No setup or platform fee. Provided as part of the package.

Vendor Costs

Billed directly to each vendor. Vendors keep sales apart from the transaction service fee.
ItemRateNotes
POS terminal KSH 2,000 / day Per vendor for the full day/evening. Waived if the vendor supplies their own Android device.
CirclePing on own device No cost CirclePing is installed on the vendor's Android device at no charge.
Transaction service fee 2% / transaction Vendor receives 98% of each sale. Attendees pay face value with no surcharge at the till.

Proposal Summary

CirclePing gives the event one connected operational layer.

The organizer's direct outlay is event staff at KSH 20,000 plus wristbands if the cost is not passed to attendees. Vendors cover their own POS where required and the 2% transaction fee. Attendees pay ticket and menu prices only.
